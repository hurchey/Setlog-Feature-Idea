# Setlog widgets

The language of the Setlog widget concept: a group of friends who each log a short moment every hour, and the widgets that show it without opening the app.

## People

**Group**:
The friends who log together and see each other's moments, such as "Roommates".
_Avoid_: Circle, team, room

**You**:
The group member whose phone the widgets are on.
_Avoid_: Me, user, owner

**Friend**:
Anyone in the group other than you.
_Avoid_: Member, contact

## Logging

**Hour**:
One hourly logging window, named by when it starts on a 24-hour clock ("14:00").
_Avoid_: Window, slot, period

**Log**:
To post your moment for the hour.
_Avoid_: Post, check in, upload

**Moment**:
A short clip someone logged in an hour, shown as a photo card with the hour and their caption.
_Avoid_: Post, log (as a noun), clip

**Reaction**:
An emoji a group member adds to a moment; each friend has their own, and yours is a heart.
_Avoid_: Like

**Nudge**:
A reminder you send to the friends who haven't logged this hour.
_Avoid_: Poke, ping

## The hour at a glance

**Hour status**:
Everything the widgets show about the current hour for you: who has logged, who's missing, what you can do next, its mood, the time left, and today's hours.
_Avoid_: This hour, hour state

**Your turn**:
The point in an hour when every friend has logged and you haven't.
_Avoid_: Last one, your move

**Busy hour**:
An hour in which three or more of the group have logged.
_Avoid_: Hot hour

**Late night**:
The hours from 23:00 to 04:59.
_Avoid_: Night mode, quiet hours

**Day bar**:
A strip of today's 24 hours that gets brighter the more of the group logged in each.
_Avoid_: Hour bar, timeline

**Recap**:
The end-of-day scrapbook of everyone's moments, shown from 22:00, ready to watch as the day's vlog.
_Avoid_: Summary, digest
